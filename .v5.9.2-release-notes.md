# ZenState 5.9.2 — More room in the menu popup

- The popup opened from the ZenState menu-bar or tray icon is taller and fits the current screen. Today and Team scroll while the footer controls stay accessible.
- Quick Record Time starts as a compact row. Click to expand it; collapsing it keeps your unfinished note. Starting a timer collapses the form again.
- Hide Quick Record Time from the popup in **Settings → General → Show Quick Record Time in the menu popup**. Dashboard and floating-timer capture are unchanged.
- Weekly allocation now compares logged hours with the Ops target through yesterday for the running week. Planned hours and the full-week target remain visible below. Past and upcoming weeks keep their existing display.

Available for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, Windows x64, and Linux x64 (AppImage and Debian).
