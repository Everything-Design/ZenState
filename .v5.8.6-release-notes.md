# ZenState 5.8.6 — Your week

- Replaces Weekly allocation with a personal Your week overview from Everything Ops.
- Shows Billable and Growth plans, logged hours, leave-adjusted targets and capacity for the selected and following week.
- Adds your project status, expected end and reported progress, personal requests/reminders, and leave conflicts.
- Keeps local unposted sessions and the live timer separate from imported Ops totals.
- Removes the local test-build notice and sample preview.

Time recording continues independently if Ops is unavailable. This overview is read-only; use Open Ops for changes.

Available for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, Windows x64, and Linux x64 (AppImage and Debian).
